Follow the instructions in the `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills together.

Run the interview as `grilling` describes. As decisions settle, record them as `domain-modeling` describes: domain terms into the `CONTEXT.md` glossary, hard-to-reverse decisions as ADRs. Write as you go, not all at the end. Do not act on the outcome until the user confirms you have reached a shared understanding.
